About TOKIWA HOUSE

TOKIWA HOUSE creates music and audio apps that make your iPhone a readily available tool for everyday music-making and instrument practice.

Our approach

Recording, editing, adjusting loudness and organizing practice may each be small tasks, but musicians repeat them often. We focus on helping you get straight to the task on your iPhone without having to set up a full production environment every time.

Giving each app a clear role makes it easier to focus on today's goal, whether that is recording, editing or practice. When we add features, we prioritize keeping the workflow easy to follow.

What you will find here

Alongside app features, we explain how the tools can fit into real music-making and introduce the basics of recording, loudness and practice. Our guides aim to be useful even if you do not use TOKIWA HOUSE apps.
