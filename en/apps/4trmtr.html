What you can do with 4trMTR

4trMTR helps you get an idea recorded before opening a full DAW. Start with a rhythm or chord part, then layer other instruments and vocals across up to four tracks.

Four-track overdubbing

Record each part separately and build a song step by step. Four tracks are a practical fit for a demo with guitar, bass, vocals and a simple rhythm.

Try it in your browser

4trMTR WEB needs no installation. Try multitrack recording in a computer browser, including on a modest PC. Performance depends on your setup.

Record on your iPhone

Capture a phrase as it comes to you, add a part to a practice recording, or make a rough demo to share with your band.

Export WAV audio

Export your recording as an audio file, ready for level adjustments, editing or sharing.

How to overdub four tracks in your browser

4trMTR WEB offers recording, playback, track levels, panning, mute, solo, project saving and WAV export. Start with a short recording and playback check, then add more tracks.

  1. Record a rhythm or chord part on the first track to establish the song.
  2. Play that track while recording another instrument or vocal on the second track.
  3. Check the balance with LEVEL, PAN, MUTE and SOLO.
  4. Save your project as you work, then export a WAV file when you are ready.

Browser recording depends on your device, browser and microphone settings. Make a short test recording before an important take.

Build a band demo on your own

A four-track limit makes it easier to decide what to record first. Here is one way to divide the parts.

Track 1

Record a rhythm, chords or a guide guitar part to establish the song.

Track 2

Add bass or another low part to support the rhythm and harmony.

Track 3

Record the main vocal, melody or lead guitar.

Track 4

Add backing vocals, another guitar, effects or an arrangement idea.

Frequently asked questions

How can I try 4trMTR?

Open 4trMTR WEB in your browser without installing software. For a dedicated iPhone app, visit the App Store listing.

Can I use it on Windows?

Yes, the web version can run on a Windows PC with a compatible browser and microphone input. Make a short test recording before an important session.

Can I use more than four tracks?

4trMTR is built around four tracks. Keeping the track count small helps keep recording song ideas and rough demos straightforward.

Can I export my recording?

4trMTR WEB supports WAV export. Use the exported file for further editing, loudness adjustment or sharing.
