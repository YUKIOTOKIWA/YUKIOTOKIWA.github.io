TRACK EDITOR

An audio editor that helps you focus on what to keep and what to refine in a recording.

Decide what the edit needs to achieve

More editing options can mean more decisions. Before you start, decide whether you need to remove unwanted material at the beginning and end, compare a section of a performance, or even out changes in volume. A clear goal helps you avoid over-editing.

Keep the original

Work with the original file preserved whenever possible. Export the finished version under a different name so that you can revisit your choices later.

Finish by listening

A waveform helps you find edit points, but playback tells you whether they sound natural. Listen carefully to transitions, gaps and sudden volume changes, including on headphones.
