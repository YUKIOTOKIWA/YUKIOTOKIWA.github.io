LUFS SLIDER

Use LUFS as a shared reference for audio loudness, alongside your own sense of whether a recording sounds loud or quiet.

Why look at LUFS?

Peak levels alone do not always tell you how loud a recording feels overall. LUFS measures loudness over time and can help you make decisions when preparing audio for distribution or matching the levels of several tracks.

A target is a reference

A suitable loudness target depends on the genre, dynamics, destination and artistic intent. Listen to the balance of the original recording as you work, rather than flattening its dynamics just to reach a number.

A practical workflow

First check the original audio, then compare its loudness with your intended target. After making adjustments, listen for changes in the bass, the clarity of vocals and instruments, and the character of brief peaks.

LUFS SLIDER WEB

Try LUFS SLIDER directly in your browser, with no installation required. Analyze integrated LUFS, set your target loudness, and adjust output level, stereo width, and limiting.

Compare original and processed audio with waveform previews, check the actual processed LUFS, and export your finished audio as a WAV file. Audio files are processed locally and never uploaded to a server.

The current app interface is in Japanese.
