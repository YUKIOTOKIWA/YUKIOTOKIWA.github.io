PRACTICE DESK

Bring a metronome, timer, sheet music and photo viewer, and recording and playback tools into one practice environment.

Reduce small interruptions

Open one app to check the tempo, switch to another to view the score, then switch again to record. Each step is small, but together they can break your concentration. PRACTICE DESK brings frequently used tools close together.

Use recordings to hear your progress

While playing, your attention is often on your hands or the next phrase. A short recording and an immediate listen can reveal timing changes or note lengths that are hard to notice during a performance.

Give each block of practice a purpose

Try ten minutes on timing, ten minutes on two difficult bars, then a complete recorded run-through. A timer can help make each part of your practice specific.
