About TOKIWA HOUSE

Record an idea. Keep a practice take. Adjust an audio file's loudness. Bring your daily accounts together. We make tools for specific tasks that you can start using straight away.

More features do not always make an app easier to use. TOKIWA HOUSE focuses on small, purposeful apps with clear workflows, manageable saved data, and a natural place in your everyday music-making and work.

Web app LUFS analysis Audio mastering

Analyze audio loudness, adjust target LUFS, stereo width and limiting, and compare original and processed audio with waveform previews. Export the finished audio as a WAV file. Your audio stays in your browser and is never uploaded to a server.
